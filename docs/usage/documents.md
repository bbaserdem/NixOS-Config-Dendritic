# Documents Organization

What type of documents are kept; and how are they organized in this guide.

Documents are kept in the xdg compliant `~/Documents` folder.

## Documents Folder Layout

## Books

My books library is managed with `calibre`.
Book reader suggested is `kavita`.

## Research

Research paper library is managed with `zotero`

## Notes

Note taking happens through `obsidian` to render notes.

## Comics

Comic books are managed seperately from books (manga and comics are together).
Proposedly to be managed with `komga`.

## Bookmarks

Want to go to self-deployed bookmark management.
Recommended `linkding` for this.
